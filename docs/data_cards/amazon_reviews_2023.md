## Labels

The background carries **no labels** (`label_source=unknown`); unknown stays unknown and never enters supervised loss or labelled metrics. Manipulation labels are **synthetic** (planted campaigns, `label_source=synthetic`), generated by `reviewring/simulation/campaigns.py` under the documented scenario grid. Simulator labels describe the simulator only.

## Known limitations

- Single category (subscription boxes), ~16k background reviews - a small, English-only cohort.
- Category-scale counts from the source listing are not post-cleaning counts.
- No verified real-world ring labels exist for this category; background alerts are unverified by construction.
